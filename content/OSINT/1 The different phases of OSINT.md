## **1️⃣ Planning & Objective Definition**  
Clearly identify what you are looking for and define key questions to guide the information collection process.

## **2️⃣ Data Collection**  
Gather information from open sources such as:  
- Search engines  
- Social networks  
- Public databases  
- Web archives  

## **3️⃣ Processing & Organization**  
- Filter and structure the collected data.  
- Verify its accuracy.  
- Eliminate irrelevant or redundant information.  

## **4️⃣ Analysis & Interpretation**  
- Correlate data to detect patterns, trends, or anomalies.  
- Extract actionable intelligence to meet the initial objectives.  

## **5️⃣ Dissemination & Reporting**  
- Summarize findings in a clear and structured report.  
- Provide actionable insights for informed decision-making.  
- Enable feedback to refine the OSINT approach.
